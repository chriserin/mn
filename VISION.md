A metronome tui, built using the golang bubbletea framework.

It should support tempo controls, tempo increases at regular intervals and at choosable tempo intervals.

It should look fun and interesting even with minimal functionality.

The project should support phased work. First, design documents in the
design directory, and the design documents should be discussed. Second,
gherkin stories in .ft files in the fts directory. Third, the ft stories
that are ready (see cli tool `ft --help`) should be implemented writing
tests first.
